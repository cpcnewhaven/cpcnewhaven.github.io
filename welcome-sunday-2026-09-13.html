Adult Class: The Wonders of Communal Worship

This class will be a great opportunity to discuss all that God promises to do through communal worship, and why we worship the way we do at CPC. We’ll study exciting theology, insight into how we’re created, principles for deciding worship styles, and more. All are welcome.
