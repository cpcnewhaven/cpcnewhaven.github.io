Adult Sunday Studies · 9:30am

The Wonders of Communal Worship

A six-week study of the God-centered worship Christ gives to his church—shaped by his Word, shared by his people, and offered together in the life of the church.

Fall 2026 · Course outline

A six-week roadmap

We will trace the theology, practices, and gospel-shaped movements of worship from gathering to benediction.

01

Overall Theology of Worship

God-centered worship, worship in general and communal worship, and the Sabbath and Lord’s Day.

02

Distinctives of Communal Worship

Elements and forms, God’s presence, and Trinitarian worship.

03

Adoration & Confession

Biblical patterns, the holiness of God, and the gospel arc.

04

Preaching of the Word

What a sermon is, why preaching matters, and its guidelines and goals.

05

The Lord’s Supper

The purpose and vision of the Supper, its practice, and its relationship to the Word.

06

Coronation & Benediction

Christ’s kingship, biblical blessing, and a review of the course.
