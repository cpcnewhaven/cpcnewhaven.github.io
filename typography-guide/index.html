Use this checklist to judge the same complete pairing across print, physical spaces, and screens. Sizes below are starting points for proofs, not fixed brand rules.

Use caseStarting size / hierarchyCheck before production
Foyer poster · 11 × 17 inTitle 60–96 pt; details 24–36 ptShort title; few details. Proof full-size in the foyer.
Outdoor signSize letters to viewing distance and sign dimensionsKeep copy short; test a physical proof at approach distance. Do not convert screen pixels directly into sign sizes.
Wayfinding / room labelsTitle 36–72 pt; secondary 18–24 ptUse arrows with short room names; check at installation height.
Sunday bulletin / handoutTitle 24–32 pt; heads 16–20 pt; body 11–12 pt / 15–18 pt leadingProof on the actual paper; offer larger text when needed.
Invitation / small cardTitle 24–36 pt; details 12–14 ptKeep practical details prominent; avoid tiny reversed text.
Website · desktopH1 48–64 px; H2 32–40 px; body 18 px / 1.6Keep paragraphs about 55–75 characters wide.
Website · mobileH1 32–40 px; H2 24–28 px; body 16–18 px / 1.6Check a 320 px viewport, wrapping, and 200% zoom.
Navigation / buttons / captionsNavigation 16 px; buttons 16–18 px; captions 14–16 pxUse the body family; distinguish controls beyond font size.
Foyer screen · 1920 × 1080Title 64–96 px; details 32–48 pxTest from the furthest seat under real lighting.
Social · 1080 px wideTitle 72–120 px; details 36–48 pxEvaluate at phone display size, not only at export resolution.
