Print Collateral

Standards: Letter (8.5 × 11 in), A4, or Tabloid (11 × 17 in).

  • Resolution: 300 dpi minimum at 100% final scale
  • Color Profile: CMYK or specified Pantone (PMS) spot colors
  • Margins & Bleed: 0.125″ bleed with 0.375″ safe margin
  • Outdoor A-Frames: 24 × 36 in (2:3 ratio) at 150–300 dpi

Digital Media

Standards: Scalable vector SVG, transparent PNG, or high-quality JPG.

  • Color Profile: sRGB for web, mobile, and social
  • Logos & Icons: Vector SVG or PNG with alpha channel
  • Sermon Slides & Displays: 16:9 ratio (1920 × 1080 px)
  • Podcast Thumbnails: 1:1 ratio (min 1400 × 1400 px, max 3000 × 3000 px)

Typography Specs

Standards: Google Fonts open-source pairing.

  • Serif: Source Serif 4 (Display, headings, scripture)
  • Sans: Poppins (UI, navigation, service times, tags)
  • Web Delivery: Embedded via Google Fonts API
  • Figma / Desktop: Available for free download on Google Fonts
