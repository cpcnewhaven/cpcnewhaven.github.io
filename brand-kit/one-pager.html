04 / Production Standards & Media Specifications Checklist for printers, signage fabricators, and web developers
🖨️ Commercial Print Specs
  • Resolution: 300 dpi minimum at 1:1 scale
  • Color Mode: CMYK or specified Pantone (PMS)
  • Bleed & Margin: 0.125″ bleed, 0.375″ safe margin
  • Paper Sizes: Letter (8.5×11″), Tabloid (11×17″), A4
  • Stock: Natural white uncoated or matte coated
🪧 Outdoor & A-Frame Signs
  • Dimensions: Standard 24″ × 36″ sandwich board (2:3)
  • Materials: Black metal architectural frame or cedar
  • Faces: White or CPC Blue Polycarbonate
  • Distance Check: 15–30 ft sidewalk legibility (Poppins)
  • Resolution: 150–300 dpi at full physical scale
💻 Digital & Screen Formats
  • Color Mode: sRGB color space for all displays
  • Sermon Slides: 16:9 ratio (1920 × 1080 px)
  • Podcast Cover: 1:1 ratio (min 1400×1400, max 3000×3000)
  • Logo Vectors: SVG format with shapes converted to paths
  • Web Transparency: 8-bit alpha PNG for raster icons
