First Time Visiting?

Here is what to expect when you join us this Sunday.

The Service

Our 10:30am service lasts about 75 minutes. We sing historic hymns and contemporary songs, confess our faith, hear scripture preached expositorily, and celebrate the Lord's Supper each week.

What to Wear

Come as you are! You will see everything from jeans and t-shirts to business casual. Wear whatever is comfortable for you.

Parking & Transit

Free on-site parking is available behind the church building, with ample free Sunday street parking along Bradley Street, Lincoln Street, and Whitney Ave. Convenient walking distance to Yale campus.

For Children

Children are always welcome in worship! A staffed nursery is available for infants and toddlers, and children's activity bulletins with crayons are available at the entrance.

Fellowship Lunch

Join us in Fellowship Hall immediately after worship for lunch! It is completely free for visitors and one of the best ways to get to know our church family and pastors.

Students & Scholars

New Haven is home to undergraduate, graduate, and international scholars. We have active student rides, mentorship, and home hospitality throughout the academic year.
